# Research Projects & Areas of Work

**Dr. Farhan Khalid** — Agronomy, experimental design and scientific computing

This overview distinguishes a **publicly documented project or publication** from **current research themes and software development**. It does not imply that unpublished data, externally funded proposals, research protocols or software releases are freely available.

## Publicly documented research

### Rice root adaptation and seed priming

**Project:** Comparative evaluation of morphological and anatomical adaptations in rice roots by seed priming under aerobic and anaerobic conditions.

**Recorded support:** Higher Education Commission (Pakistan), Start-up Research Grant Program (2015–2016), as described on the [IUB faculty profile](https://iub.edu.pk/author/farhan.khalid).

**Research focus:** Rice-root development, priming, aerenchyma and contrasting soil-water regimes.

### Rice establishment and system of rice intensification

**Published study (2022):** Comparison of direct dry-seeding and seed priming alongside system-of-rice-intensification and conventional establishment practices. [Read DOI record](https://doi.org/10.1080/15427528.2021.1910094).

### Wheat under saline and heavy-metal conditions

**Published study (2026):** Combined glutathione and moringa leaf extract treatments, antioxidant defence and nutrient balance under heavy-metal-contaminated saline soil. [Read DOI record](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00299-026-04010-5).
