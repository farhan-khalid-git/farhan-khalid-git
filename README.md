## About me

I am a **Lecturer in Agronomy** at **The Islamia University of Bahawalpur, Pakistan**, with a PhD in Agronomy. My work connects field-crop research with rigorous experimental design, statistical analysis and reproducible research reporting. I am especially interested in making agronomic data easier to validate, analyse, visualise and interpret for scientific publication and practical crop management.

I use **R** as a principal research-analysis environment and work with **R Markdown/Quarto**, **VS Code** and **Codex-assisted R/Python workflows** for analysis, modelling, visualisation and research-software development. These are AI-assisted development workflows; they should not be read as a claim of independently verified proficiency in every programming or modelling technique.

## Statistical analysis and research computing

- **Experimental design:** CRD, RCBD, factorial, split-plot and more complex field-study structures, with analysis matched to the actual randomisation and replication.
- **Statistical workflows:** ANOVA, mixed-effects models, model diagnostics, treatment comparisons and suitable multiple-comparison procedures.
- **Multivariate exploration:** PCA, correlation structures, clustering and heatmaps where scientifically appropriate.
- **Crop growth and modelling:** Growth indices, temporal response modelling and evaluation of growth equations.
- **Research outputs:** Dataset quality checks, traceable calculations, publication-style tables, figures and reproducible reports.
- **Tools and workflow:** R, Quarto/R Markdown, RStudio, VS Code and Codex-assisted R/Python scripting.

## Research software and reproducible workflows

### 🌱 `cropGrowthR` — R package under development

I am developing **cropGrowthR**, a research-software project focused on crop-growth analysis. The intended scope brings together growth equations, experimental-design-aware statistical analysis, functional modelling, source-traceable calculations and publication-ready results.

**Status:** Development and verification work is ongoing. Completion of coding stages does **not** by itself establish independent numerical validation, package quality or readiness for a public release. A public repository/release will be linked here when available and verified.

### 📄 Reproducible scientific manuscripts
