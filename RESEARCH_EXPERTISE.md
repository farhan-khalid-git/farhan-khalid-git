# Agronomy & Research Expertise

**Dr. Farhan Khalid** — Lecturer, Department of Agronomy, The Islamia University of Bahawalpur, Pakistan.

## Agronomy

- **Crop production:** Establishment, sowing methods, seed priming, spacing and stand management in wheat, rice and maize.
- **Crop physiology and growth:** Phenology, plant-growth indices, biomass production, yield components and resource use.
- **Abiotic stress:** Drought, terminal heat and salinity-related agronomic constraints.
- **Plant nutrition:** Fertiliser management, foliar interventions, nutrient-use efficiency and soil–crop relationships.
- **Biological and organic inputs:** Plant-growth-promoting microorganisms, inoculants, biofertilisers and organic soil amendments.
- **Cropping systems:** Plant population, maize–soybean intercropping and sustainable field-crop management.

## Experimentation and research interpretation

My research approach combines agronomic questions with design-aware statistical analysis. Key areas include CRD, RCBD, factorial and split-plot studies; diagnostic assessment; treatment interactions; mixed-effects models; and appropriate multivariate exploration.

I emphasise independent experimental units, suitable randomisation, biological plausibility, defensible mean comparisons and uncertainty reporting.

[Read the detailed statistical workflow](STATISTICAL_EXPERTISE.md).

## Scientific computing

**R**, RStudio, R Markdown, Quarto and VS Code are central to my research workflows. I also use **Codex within VS Code** for AI-assisted R/Python scripting, analysis development and research software work. Assisted code requires independent checking against study design and trusted calculations.
