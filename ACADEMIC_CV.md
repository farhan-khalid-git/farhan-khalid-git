## Current appointment

**Lecturer in Agronomy**, The Islamia University of Bahawalpur, Pakistan.

My academic work includes undergraduate and postgraduate teaching, student research supervision and agronomic experimentation. Subject areas include field-crop production, crop physiology, nutrient management, field experimentation and agricultural research methods.

## Areas of research

- Wheat, rice and maize production and crop-establishment methods.
- Drought, heat and salinity stress management in semi-arid environments.
- Plant nutrition, biofertilisers, organic amendments and microbial inoculants.
- Cropping-system management, plant density and intercropping.
- Crop-growth analysis, experimental design and reproducible statistical reporting.

## Research computing

- **R and RStudio:** Experimental data analysis, modelling, diagnostics and graphics.
- **R Markdown and Quarto:** Reproducible analytical reporting and manuscripts.
- **Visual Studio Code + Codex:** AI-assisted R/Python research scripts, analytical workflows and software development, with independent checking of scientific results.
- **Experimental inference:** CRD, RCBD, factorial and split-plot studies; appropriate ANOVA, mixed modelling and multivariate exploration.
